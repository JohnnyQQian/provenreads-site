Delete your ProvenReads account

ProvenReads is operated by Qiang Qian.

Open Account → Delete account in the app and confirm. This deletes your account, child profiles, quiz records, reading history and saved book preferences. Deletion is permanent.

Once your deletion request is accepted, account access stops while deletion completes. Temporary outages may delay removal of the data.

Cannot access the app? Email support@provenreads.com from your account email address to request deletion. We verify ownership first. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

For a guest account, request deletion from the device where you use it. If you lose access to that device, we may be unable to identify your guest account.

To remove only child data, use Account → Delete children data instead.

Our temporary sign-in and abuse-prevention records expire within seven days and are cleared during regular cleanup. Privacy policy.
