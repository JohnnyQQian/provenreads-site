ProvenReads privacy policy

Updated 7 November 2026. ProvenReads is operated by Qiang Qian for parents and caregivers. The app provides ideas for children's books based on trusted awards and reading lists from the US, UK, Australia and Singapore.

What we collect and why

  • Your email, if you choose email sign-in, and an account identifier to save and restore your household.
  • Child profile details you provide, such as nickname, age, gender, avatar and interests. These details help us recommend books suited to your child.
  • Quiz activity and results, reading history, and liked, finished or hidden books to personalize recommendations and track progress.
  • Connection information, such as IP addresses, to deliver the service and prevent abuse.

Services and external links

Service providers help us manage accounts, store information and deliver book information. They receive the information needed for those services. We use encrypted connections and protected storage for sign-in details.

Library and shopping links open other services with their own privacy policies. The app has no third-party advertising or analytics.

Library links and Amazon links are provided for your convenience. As an Amazon Associate, the ProvenReads app earns from qualifying purchases.

Your choices and deletion

You can use a guest account without an email, edit child profiles, delete child data, or delete your account. Guest access may be lost if you lose access to your device.

We keep account and reading data until you delete it or your account. Account deletion removes associated child profiles and reading data. Temporary outages may delay completion. Our temporary sign-in and abuse-prevention records expire within seven days and are cleared during regular cleanup.

How to delete your account. For privacy questions or requests, email support@provenreads.com.
